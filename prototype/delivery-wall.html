Tips so your money arrives without delays

  • Pay from an account in your own nameTransfers from friends, relatives or companies get stopped by the banks’ compliance checks and sent back, which takes weeks.
  • Put your order number in the referenceWrite it exactly as shown, e.g. TMLG-2026-0417. Then we can match your payment the same day.
  • Raise your transfer limit before the 90%Many banks cap international transfers at a few thousand euros a day. Ask your bank or change the limit in its app a week ahead.
  • Upload the transfer receiptAdd the bank’s confirmation (for SWIFT, the MT103) in your portal. We can prepare the handover while the money is still on its way.
  • Compare the rate before you choose a currencyIf your bank’s euro rate is poor, pay in local currency. That way the money is converted once, not twice.
  • Our bank details never changeCopy them only from your portal. If anyone sends you “new” details by WhatsApp or email, it’s fraud. Call your manager before you pay.
